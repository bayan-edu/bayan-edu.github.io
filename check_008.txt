A long ship sails on the deep sea.
The big ship carries a lot of cargo.
Men work on the open deck of the ship.
The captain looks out from the high deck.
Big waves hit the side of the ship.
The ship stays strong in the rough sea.
At night, bright lamps shine on the deck.
The ship heads to the busy port.
Tug boats guide the ship to the dock.
The long ship rests at the safe port.
